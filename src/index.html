About

I'm a product manager with 4 years of experience across healthcare, marketplaces, telco, edtech, legal tech and payments. At Novac Payments, I own the Business Mobile App roadmap and managed the new onboarding flow for the web and mobile app.

Thinking

How I work through a product problem.

The process behind the results above.

1

Name the number

Before anything is built, I define what number has to move: the success metrics and OKRs the work will be judged on.

2

Find the friction

Funnel and drop-off analysis, customer feedback and user research show where users and operations teams get stuck.

3

Write it down

PRDs, user stories and engineering-ready specifications with acceptance criteria.

4

Build and test with the team

Sprint playback and Scrum ceremonies with engineering and design. I write test cases, run functional testing and coordinate UAT and release sign-off myself.

−15% post-launch bugs on my releases
5

Launch, then keep measuring

Funnel, adoption, retention and churn analysis after launch decide what goes into the next sprint.

Two pieces where the deliverable was the thinking itself.

A diagnosis and a teardown, both done from the outside with no internal context handed to me.

Legal tech · Law Pavilion

Primsol: finding the real problem

NPS was falling — stale search, missing judgments, broken mobile, and AI features with 68% trial but only 11% sustained use. I worked the data back to one cause: users try Primsol but don't trust it enough to rely on it. Reliability was the gap, not activation.

  • Diagnose 3 symptoms → 1 root cause
  • Validate 5–7 user conversations
  • Recommend Fix reliability, defer redesign
